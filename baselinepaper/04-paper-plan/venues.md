# Venues for the baseline / negative-result paper

Checked on **2026-11-10** by web search plus a read of each venue's official page. Paper: a short empirical,
partly negative-result study (autoencoder anomaly detection vs supervised models vs the rule-based plausibility
baseline for Sybil detection on VeReMi-Extension; evaluation methodology: vehicle-level splits, leakage, shortcut
baselines).

**How to read the "Verified" column**

- **official** — the date / fact was read on the venue's own page on 2026-11-10 (URL in the last column).
- **official, conflict** — two official pages of the same venue disagree; both values are given.
- **partly** — the deadline is official, but some other fields (page limit, indexing, review model) were not on the
  pages opened; those fields say "not stated".
- **unverified** — the official page could not be opened (blocked) or the next call is not posted; any value given
  comes from a third-party listing or the last edition and is marked as such.

Nothing here is a guess: "not stated" means the page opened did not say it. Deadlines move (extensions are common
at VTC, VNC, CNS); re-check the official URL before committing.

## Recommendation

1. **USENIX VehicleSec '27, Cycle 1 — 15 Dec 2026 (short / WIP, 6 pages).** The only venue whose whole audience is vehicle / V2X security; double-blind; artifact evaluation rewards the released splits and code; a "major revision" rolls into Cycle 2 (11 Mar 2027). Cost: not IEEE Xplore, one full-price registration.
2. **IEEE SoutheastCon 2027 — 30 / 31 Dec 2026 (6 pages).** In Daytona Beach, Florida (cheap travel from Florida Poly), Cybersecurity track, double-blind rolling review with a decision within 20 days, IEEE Xplore; the lowest-risk route to a publication by spring. It refuses work already posted as a preprint, so no arXiv first.
3. **IEEE VTC2027-Spring — S2R-CAM workshop (15 / 20 Dec 2026) or Recent Results (1 Feb 2027).** Vehicular-technology audience, 5–7 pages, IEEE VTS; the February date is the realistic one if December slips. Cost: travel to Hamburg and US$100 per page over 5.
4. One paper can be under review at one venue only: pick 1 **or** 2 for December, keep 3 (February) and VehicleSec Cycle 2 / ITSC 2027 (1 Mar 2027) / MetaCRiSP 2027 (date not announced) as the next stops.
5. **Fallback:** a journal version — **TMLR** (no fee stated, judges evidence, not novelty) or **IEEE Access** (negative results explicitly in scope, US$2,160) — plus an arXiv preprint once the chosen venue's preprint rule allows it. Avoid Computers & Security (AI/ML moratorium).
