[0.538, 0.579]) and the three pseudonym-rotating attacks strongly: 0.952 [0.948, 0.955] for DataReplay and 0.967

20-vehicle rule; for DataReplay it is reportable (length-only 0.927). The percentile operating points of the
control are degenerate (more than 5\% of benign validation windows have one row, so no window exceeds the
threshold); the control is read through its AUROC.

\subsection{Preliminary Evidence From Earlier Single-Seed Runs}\label{sec:res-prelim}

\todo{This subsection and Table~\ref{tab:prelim} exist only so that the draft shows the expected shape of the
results. They will be removed from the final paper. Source: 01-our-studies/inventory.md.}
Before this protocol we ran nine small studies with the same family of convolutional autoencoder. They are
\emph{not} results of this paper: they use different window lengths (32, 48, 64), different splits and test
vehicles, different normalizations, and, except for one study with three seeds, a single seed without intervals
(Table~\ref{tab:prelim}, blue). Their numbers cannot be compared across rows. They motivated the regimes and the
length control: reconstruction trained on benign data scored \prelim{0.655--0.736} on GridSybil across the three
window lengths, an unlabeled mix scored \prelim{0.482--0.484} where the attack is the majority of the training
windows and \prelim{0.610} where it is 31\%, GridSybil labels gave \prelim{0.926--0.929}, and on DataReplay and
DoSDisruptive the benign-only autoencoder (\prelim{0.391}, \prelim{0.611}) scored below window length alone
(0.952, 0.967). The contrast between the 31\% and the 64\% mix is suggestive only: these runs also differ in
window length, input, split and test vehicles. The controlled version is the mix-all and mix-gridfolders regimes
of this protocol (C2, C3), which are themselves two confounded points; a sweep over the attack share is out of
scope (Section~\ref{sec:scope}).
