## Common gaps

1. **Identity is taken at face value.** Per-identity tests, trust and consensus (B07, B08, B01, A18, A22, A24, C22) treat each pseudonym as an independent agent. A Sybil attacker gets several votes and resets trust with every new pseudonym. Only A09, A19, A33, A34, B35 / C18 and C01 try to link identities, and they need protocol changes (location proofs, co-location attestations, RSSI) or use unnamed data.
2. **Cooperation shares models, not evidence.** Federated MBD (A01, A02, A21, A25, A30, A35, A36) shares weights; few papers share per-message verdicts across receivers of the same broadcast, although VeReMi-Extension logs every receiver of every broadcast.
3. **Reporters are assumed honest.** Collaborative detectors rarely let the attackers lie in the collaboration itself. Exceptions study FL poisoning (A03, A26, C05–C08, C32, C34) or label flipping between RSUs (A04), not Sybil vehicles filing false misbehaviour reports.
4. **Evaluation on private simulations.** Of 109 papers, only 10 use the VeReMi family; most Sybil papers use their own SUMO / ns-2 / OMNeT++ runs (A07, A08, A10, A20, A24) with no released code (3 of 36 in area A have code). Results are not comparable.
5. **Leakage is not discussed.** No VeReMi paper we found reports a split by physical vehicle or the cross-scenario near-copies (our F1, F14). High accuracies (A05 F1 0.98, A06 98%) are therefore hard to compare.
6. **Sybil subsets are under-studied.** Even on VeReMi-Extension, work concentrates on position / speed falsification and DoS; DataReplaySybil (paper F1 ≈ 0.51) and GridSybil have almost no multi-agent detector.
7. **Traffic MARL and crowdsensing MARL ignore adversaries.** MARL signal control (B23–B26), LLM traffic agents (B27–B31) and MARL worker scheduling (C09–C12, C25–C28, C30, C35) assume truthful reports, while B33, B34, B35 and C04 show that one Sybil or colluding vehicles can distort them.
8. **Sybil-proof mechanisms do not detect.** Crowdsensing Sybil-proof auctions (C15, C16, C33) remove the incentive to create identities but give no detector; truth discovery under Sybils (C01, C31, C36) works on labels / static readings, not motion.
